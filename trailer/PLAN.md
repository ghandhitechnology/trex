# trex trailer plan

75 s, 1920x1080 at 60 fps, 128 BPM, 40 bars (bar = 1.875 s, beat = 0.46875 s).
Everything is code: pixel scenes drawn per frame on a 320x180 buffer (6x, no smoothing),
gameplay captured by the game's own renderer, music and SFX synthesized by a script.

## Look

A game trailer, never a website. No cards, panels, centered text on flat
backgrounds, or UI chrome. Every frame has motion under it: gameplay, a drawn
world, parallax, particles. Text is minimal (1-3 words), in the TREX-logo pixel
style (`PX.title`), and always lands ON a beat with a hit (drop-in, slam, shake,
flash, sparks). Cuts land on downbeats. Stop-motion character animation is
stepped at 12 fps (`PX.step(t)`); camera, shake and particles run at 60.

## Engine (engine/px.js) — do not edit; add helpers in your own files

- `PX.scene({id, start, end, layer, draw(t, local), cues})`: `t` global seconds, `local = t - start`.
- `PX.bar(n, beat)`: start time of bar n (1-based) + beat offset. `PX.BEAT`, `PX.BAR`.
- Drawing: `PX.rect/px/line/circle/dither/clear`, raw context `PX.g`, palette `PX.P`.
- Sprites: `PX.spr(name, x, y, frame, {k, flip, alpha, flash})`, names from `assets/sprites/sprites.json` (+ `logo`).
- Text: `PX.title(str, cx, y, {k, bands, fx, shine})` big logo-style; `PX.text(str, x, y, color, {k, align})` small 3x5 game font.
  Effects: `PX.dropIn(age)`, `PX.slamScale(age)`, `PX.step(t, fps)`.
- Particles and hits are stateless: `PX.burst(age, x, y, opts)`, `PX.ring(age, x, y, opts)`, `PX.shake(age, amp)`.
- Randomness only via `PX.hash(i, seed)` or `PX.rng(seed)`. No Math.random, no Date, no performance.now.
- Footage: `PX.footage(id, local, {k, cx, cy, dx, dy})`, `PX.footageFrame(id, local)` -> Image. Index: `assets/footage/index.json`.
- Extra images: call `PX.load(key, src)` at script load time (before boot).

## Cues (SFX)

Each scene lists its sound effects in `cues: [[t, name, gain], ...]` with ABSOLUTE
times in seconds. `tools/cues.mjs` collects them; the music script mixes them.
Names: `key` (keyboard click), `enter` (return key), `type` (text tick),
`whoosh`, `riser`, `slam` (big title/logo hit), `impact` (heavy boom), `land`
(small letter landing), `pop` (character appears), `shine` (sparkle),
`blip` (UI move), `select` (card pick), `bone` (currency), `zap` (lightning),
`boom` (explosion), `dash`, `glitch`, `pause` (pause chime + tape-stop), `done` (agent done chime).

## Footage (assets/footage, generated by tools/capture.sh)

`index.json`: `{ "<id>": { "fps": 30, "frames": N, "w": W, "h": H, "shows": "..." } }`,
frames at `<id>/0000.png`, 1x native pixels. Full-screen shots are 320x180.
Pane shots (`pane-*`) are captured at the small tmux-pane size.

## Checking your work

Snapshot frames: `npx hyperframes snapshot --at 1.0,2.5 --no-end --describe false -o <your scratch dir>`
from `trailer/`. Look at the PNGs. Always use your own output dir.

## Build

`tools/build.sh` rebuilds everything: `tools/capture.sh` (footage), `tools/audio.sh --force`
(music, cues, mix), the 1080p60 render, and a 4K master in `renders/`.
Footage, audio, and renders are generated, so they are not committed.
